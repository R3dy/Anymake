## Elevator Pitch
Bookkeeping for freelancers who hate bookkeeping.

## Problem
Freelancers lose hours a month reconciling invoices by hand, and existing tools
are priced for agencies.

## Solution
A hosted web app that imports bank transactions, matches them to invoices, and
produces a quarterly tax summary.

## Target Audience
**Primary user:** solo freelancers billing under $150k/year.

## Scope

### MVP — In Scope
- Bank import, invoice matching, quarterly summary export
- Sign up, log in, billing portal

### Never Building
- Payroll
- Multi-entity consolidation

## Revenue Model
**Primary model:** Subscription.

**Pricing (draft):**
| Tier | Price | For |
|------|-------|-----|
| Solo | $9/month | One user, one bank account |
| Pro | $19/month | Multiple accounts, tax export |

We charge from day one — there is no free tier. First paying customers come
from the freelancer communities I'm already in.

## Success Definition
50 paying customers and $500 MRR within 90 days of launch.
